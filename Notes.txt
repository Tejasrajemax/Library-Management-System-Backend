Object-Relational Mapping (ORM) is a programming technique used to convert data between two incompatible systems:
                        object-oriented programming (OOP) languages (like Java, C#, or Python) and relational databases (like MySQL, PostgreSQL, or Oracle).

ORM acts as a translator. Instead of writing raw database queries inside your
                        application code, you can interact with your database using the native objects of your programming language.
